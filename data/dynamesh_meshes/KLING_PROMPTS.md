# Kling prompts — dynamesh (18 assets)

Every prompt carries the same four locks: fixed camera, rigid geometry with a
pixel-identical silhouette, unchanging flat-white background, and the effect
confined strictly inside the silhouette (nothing emitted into the air). Only the
surface COLOUR is allowed to change — that is what makes the output usable as GT
for a fixed-mesh dynamic-texture fit.

Render for each: `PNG/<name>.png`   Mesh: `OBJ/<name>.obj`

## 1. skull — molten lava

```
Static tripod framing; the camera is locked — no pan, tilt, roll, dolly, push-in, pull-out, zoom, reframing, or shake. The skull is a single rigid stone object: no jaw movement, no tilting, no settling, no deformation, no micro-jitter; its pose, scale, position and silhouette stay pixel-identical in every frame. The background is flat pure white and never changes — no gradient, no shadow, no haze, no embers, no particles anywhere outside the object. The effect lives strictly on the surface and never crosses the silhouette edge; nothing is emitted into the air, nothing drips off, nothing glows into the white background. The only thing that changes is the colour of the surface itself. Molten lava glows inside deep cracks running across the skull — orange-red magma slowly creeping and pulsing along the fissures, brightening to yellow-white at random points then dimming, while the dark charred crust between the cracks shifts from black to deep grey and new hairline cracks open in unpredictable places.
```
