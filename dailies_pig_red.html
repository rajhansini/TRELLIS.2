Five columns — ground truth, frozen TRELLIS.2, rung19 (cross-attention LoRA), rung27 (+ self-attention LoRA), and rung27 + MCFM temporal-only (3-frame window, v2_D). Batch G ran all three arms, so the ladder is readable left to right within one clip.
This clip starts from the grey mesh. Kling was handed the untextured unicorn render and the texture ARRIVES over the 150 frames — frame 1 is bare geometry, not a textured still being kept in motion.
Camera — fixed per clip; every frame-to-frame change is texture, never camera motion.
Ground truth away from the training view shows the driving-video frame dimmed — there is no ground truth from an angle the camera never shot.
Alignment — this clip was rejected once, wrongly. The first solve searched rotation ALONE, with scale fixed, and reported IoU 0.711; the clip was written up as "silhouette redrawn". It was not.
