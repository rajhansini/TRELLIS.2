# Kling prompts — supplementary meshes

v2 skeleton (the Dale one): seven named blocks, then the effect sentence. Per-mesh
slots filled every time — the articulations this mesh could plausibly perform, the
features that must stay readable, and any through-hole.

Input image for each: `front/<name>.png` (grey render, camera already chosen).

---

## goat — `front/goat.png`  (up +Y, yaw 160, pitch 5, 35.4% exposed)

Through-hole slot for this mesh: the gaps between the four legs and under the belly.
Unlocked, Kling fills them the way it filled the vase's handle loop.

Readable-features slot: horns, ears, beard, muzzle, hooves, leg separation, tail.

### 1. dried mud caking (live goat, farm)

Mud is a plausible liquid, so the dry-material blocks are FRONT-LOADED and the effect
is named as already-set earth. Without this it runs off the belly — same failure as the
oil and rust prompts.

```
THERE IS NO LIQUID IN THIS IMAGE. Nothing is wet, nothing flows, nothing soaks, nothing pools. Gravity has no effect on anything in this image.

ABSOLUTELY NOTHING DRIPS: no drips, runs, rivulets, streaks, teardrops, strands, tendrils, stalactites or blobs anywhere, and none hang below the belly, the beard, the tail or the hooves. The lower edges of the goat stay razor-sharp, with pure white immediately below them.

Static tripod framing; the camera is locked — no pan, tilt, roll, dolly, push-in, pull-out, zoom, reframing, or shake.
THE BACKGROUND NEVER CHANGES: every pixel outside the goat's outline is the same solid uniform white as the input image, in every single frame from first to last — flat, empty, untouched, with no gradient, no shadow, no tint, no dust.
THE SIZE NEVER CHANGES: the goat is a single rigid figurine: no breathing, swaying, head turn, ear flick, tail swish, beard sway, chewing, leg movement, no deformation, no micro-jitter. Its outline, scale and position in the frame are pixel-identical to the input image in every frame.
NO WRITING OF ANY KIND: there are no letters, glyphs, symbols, characters, numerals, inscriptions or logos anywhere on the goat. Only abstract pattern.
THE FORM MUST STAY READABLE: the goat keeps the exact sculpted lighting, shading and specular highlights of the input image. The horns, ears, beard, muzzle, hooves and the separation between the legs stay clearly visible in every frame, and at least half the coat stays bare at all times.
THE PATTERN GROWS, IT DOES NOT SLIDE: the pattern never scrolls, drifts, translates, or marches across the surface in any direction. Each patch appears at a fixed point and expands outward from that point, staying anchored where it started while its edges advance.
THE EFFECT NEVER LEAKS: everything is on the surface, flat against it, contained entirely inside the outline. Nothing crosses the outline, nothing extends past the edge, nothing drips, flakes, bleeds, spills or radiates into the background, and nothing is emitted into the air. The gaps between the legs and under the belly stay flat pure white.
The only thing that changes is the colour of the surface inside the outline. Dust-dry cracked earth cakes the coat — dull grey-brown crusts already set hard at scattered fixed points on the flanks, chest, haunches and lower legs, each patch widening outward from where it started and paling to chalky buff as it dries, dark hairline cracks opening across the dried plates in branching networks that lengthen a little each frame, clean pale coat still showing between the patches as new crusts set and craze in turn.
```
